Privacy Policy & Data Sovereignty

🏛️ Privacy Policy (গোপনীয়তা নীতি)

Effective Date: January 2026 • MKRWeb

1. 100% Offline Data Sovereignty

Sunnah 24 operates entirely offline on your device. Your habit tracker completions, Tasbih counts, and Muhasabah journal entries are stored locally on your device storage (`localStorage`). No personal data is ever transmitted or uploaded to any external corporate or cloud server.

2. Cryptographic Tamper-Proof Vault

Your local ledger state is secured using a SHA-256 cryptographic hashing engine to ensure data integrity and prevent unauthorized local script modification.

3. Zero Trackers & Zero Ads

Sunnah 24 contains zero third-party analytics trackers, zero advertising SDKs, and requires no user account creation or login.
