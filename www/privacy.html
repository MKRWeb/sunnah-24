Privacy Policy & Data Sovereignty

🏛️ Privacy Policy (গোপনীয়তা নীতি)

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Developer: MKRWeb (Package ID: com.mkrweb.sunnah24)

1. 100% Offline Data Sovereignty & Zero Data Collection

Sunnah 24 ("the Application"), developed by MKRWeb, operates 100% offline on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store, share, or sell any personal data, habit completion logs, digital Tasbih counts, or location preferences on any external server or cloud database. All information you generate inside the Application remains strictly on your physical device.

2. Local Storage & Triple-Layer Cryptographic Vault

Your daily Sunnah habit tracking records, Masnoon Adhkar progress, custom habits, and Tasbih counts are stored locally on your device using browser/WebView localStorage, IndexedDB, and the Android internal application sandbox (getFilesDir()). Local records are verified using a SHA-256 cryptographic hash to protect data integrity against accidental corruption.

3. Geographic Location & Prayer Time Calculation

To calculate accurate seasonal Islamic prayer times (Waqt) and prohibited prayer windows, Sunnah 24 uses your manual city/district selection (such as Bangladesh's 64 districts or global cities) or your device's local IANA timezone. If location access is ever granted by the user, coordinates are processed strictly on-device for astronomical solar calculations and are never transmitted over the network.

4. Data Retention & User Data Deletion Policy

Because MKRWeb does not collect or store your data on any remote server, you retain 100% control over your data retention and deletion at all times:

• In-App Reset: You can clear your daily active progress at any time using the "Reset Today" (আজকের রিসেট) button inside the app.
• Complete Data Erasure: You can permanently erase all historical records, custom habits, and vault data at any time by going to your device's Settings → Apps → Sunnah 24 → Storage → Clear Storage / Clear Data, or by uninstalling the Application.
• User-Controlled Backups: If you export a signed JSON backup file to your device's Downloads folder, that file remains under your personal file-system control until you delete it.

5. Zero Third-Party Trackers, SDKs & Advertisements

Sunnah 24 contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero behavioral analytics trackers, and requires no user account registration or login. We do not use Google Advertising ID (AD_ID).

6. Children's Privacy

Sunnah 24 does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction). Because the Application functions entirely offline without data collection, it is safe for general audiences.
